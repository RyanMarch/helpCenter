Sharing Designs

Collaborate and showcase your work by sharing live designs.

Icon Studio provides direct sharing methods to send designs to teammates or load pre-configured templates dynamically.

Shareable Links

To generate a unique URL containing your exact current configuration:

  1. Click the Share Design button in the primary actions section.
  2. The current layout, color parameters, and configuration properties are compressed into query parameters and copied to your clipboard automatically.
  3. Send this link to anyone to let them view and edit the exact design.
